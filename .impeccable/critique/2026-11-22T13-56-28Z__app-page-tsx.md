## Priority issues

### P1: Show the real community and its outcomes

The page explains its beliefs, recurring programmes and event history without showing member work, participant experiences or the people responsible for welcoming newcomers. Event posters demonstrate that events exist; they do not show what attendance feels like or what someone gains.

Add actual gathering photographs with short captions, two or three member projects with links, authentic attributed member quotations, and named organisers or hosts. Add impact numbers only when verified. Replace some repeated value exposition with evidence, so the page does not simply get longer.

Suggested command: $impeccable shape.

### P1: Bring practical reasons to join forward

In app/page.tsx the order is hero, who, vision, mission, pillars, culture, programmes, events, join, FAQ, close. A new student must scroll past several values sections before seeing practical activities. The hero speaks broadly about a student community while app/content/site.ts:39 calls it an AI student builder community.

Clarify whether AI building is the core identity or one part of a broader community. Keep the motto, then give one concrete sentence about who participates and what they do. Move a compact next-event summary and community proof near the top. Lead the navigation with activities, gatherings and joining; combine the charter material lower down.

Suggested commands: $impeccable clarify and $impeccable layout.

### P1: Complete the newcomer and provincial participation journey

app/content/site.ts:139-151 says attend Open Table, get two endorsements, then complete a short review. It does not explain who makes introductions, how endorsements are requested, how the review starts, or what the applicant should expect next. The FAQ says most meetings are in Metro Manila despite the emphasis on provinces and underserved students.

Explain how someone who knows nobody gets welcomed, how endorsement and review actually work, and who to contact. State whether there is an online, provincial or alternative introductory route; if there is none, say what participation is currently possible. Do not invent programmes or promise timelines without confirmation. Distinguish free membership from attendance expenses and any separately priced programmes.

Suggested commands: $impeccable onboard and $impeccable clarify.

### P2: Improve reading and discovery on smaller screens

The browser inspector measured 14px for the most common running-text size, although the base body token is 16px. Mission, pillar and programme rails show portions of adjacent cards at phone and tablet widths. This is intentional scrolling, not proof of page overflow, but it hides informational content behind another gesture.

Use 16-18px for substantive paragraphs, keep small text for metadata, and test stacking or compact disclosure for important informational sections. Keep the prominent gold action and bottom event dock. The hero wraps onto three lines at 390px but remains readable; this is lower priority than content and onboarding. Verify keyboard, zoom, touch gestures and screen-reader behavior separately before making compliance claims.

Suggested commands: $impeccable typeset and $impeccable adapt.

### P2: Make event freshness and empty states explicit

app/content/events.ts:80-100 calls an undocumented Luma endpoint, refreshes every six hours, and switches both periods to a bundled snapshot if either call fails. The snapshot captured on 19 November labels the 29 November gathering as future. getEvents sorts the future bucket without reclassifying entries by time, so a fallback can keep an ended event in the upcoming list. This is a future failure mode, not evidence that the current 29 November event is already stale.

Classify normalized events against current start/end times, preserve available live data per period, add a bounded fetch timeout and visible no-upcoming guidance, and log fallback use. Use a maintained last-known-good snapshot or supported feed where available. Keep stale reservations out of the bottom dock and structured data too. Add targeted date-boundary/fallback coverage when implementing the fix.

Suggested command: $impeccable harden.

## Smaller gaps

- No analytics instrumentation was found in the repository. Hosting-level analytics may exist and were not accessible. Measure event CTA clicks, joining-section visits and outbound RSVP clicks; verify completed registrations through Luma if supported rather than treating a click as a completed RSVP.
- No public team/host introduction, dedicated email contact or code-of-conduct/reporting route was found on the page. Add proportionate trust and accountability information.
- The programme description says Open Table runs 2-7 PM, while the current event is listed at noon. Label general timing as typical and make event-specific details authoritative.
- Quality checks run in the tag-triggered release workflow; no PR-triggered build/type-check or targeted event-fallback tests were found. Add focused checks when changing the integration.
- The inspector flags three fonts, gold outlines, separators and ornamental marks. These are established brand choices and are not automatic reasons to replace the visual system. Its stale Boska direction-file warning is irrelevant to the current approved brand. Sticky-header coverage at arbitrary scroll positions is not sufficient evidence of inaccessible headings.

## Recommended order

Hero with clear positioning → next gathering → community photographs and member outcomes → programmes → how to join → concise values → FAQ and contact.

Start with the three P1 issues, harden event freshness, then finish with $impeccable polish. Additional dashboards, logins or gamification are not needed to solve the observed gaps.
